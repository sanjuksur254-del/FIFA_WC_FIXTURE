🔰 FOOTBALL BEGINNER SIMPLE GUIDE

🏁 The Offside Rule

An attacker cannot stay closer to the goal line than the second-to-last opponent player (usually the last defender) at the exact split-second the ball is passed forward.

⏱️ Match Duration

Games last 90 minutes. In Group Stages, results can end in a draw. In knockout rounds, a draw triggers 30-minutes extra time, then a Penalty Shootout.

🟨 Discipline Cards

Yellow is an official warning. Two yellows equal a Red (ejection). Red carded players are kicked off immediately, leaving their team to play at a player disadvantage.

🏔️ ALTITUDE STAGE Estadio Azteca in Mexico City sits at 2,240 meters above sea level. This high altitude makes breathing very hard for visiting players, and makes the soccer ball fly faster.
